Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

Orakl is built to run entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

The short version

  • No data collection. Orakl does not gather personal data of any kind.
  • No account required. You never sign up or log in.
  • Everything stays on your device. Your questions, predictions and history never leave your iPhone.
  • No tracking or analytics. We use no third‑party trackers, advertising IDs, or profiling.

Information we collect

None. Orakl does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal or usage information. The questions you ask and the predictions Orakl generates are processed locally and saved only in your app's private on‑device storage.

On‑device intelligence

On supported devices, Orakl uses Apple's on‑device intelligence (Foundation Models) to enrich readings. This processing happens entirely on your device. Your content is not sent to Orakl or to any third party for this purpose.

Purchases

Optional Orakl Premium subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store using your Apple Account. Orakl never sees or stores your payment details. Purchase validation is performed on‑device via Apple's StoreKit. Apple's handling of your transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

Orakl includes no third‑party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children's privacy

Orakl is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Data retention & deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, you are always in control. Deleting the app removes your prediction history and all associated local data. There is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
