Running the simulation

It is taking a long time
The swarm is computed on your device, so an older iPhone takes longer than a new Mac. Keeping the app in the foreground lets it use the full processor; switching away suspends it.
The result changed when I ran it again
Expected. The agents are seeded with different opposing priors each run. A decision whose result swings wildly between runs is genuinely close — the split figure is telling you that.
The analysis missed something obvious
Orakl only knows what you typed. Add it under “Add context” and run it again; the factor list will change accordingly.
Confidence is low
That is a real signal, not an error. It means the arguments were poorly separated on the information available.

Purchases and devices

Restoring a purchase
Settings inside the app → Restore Purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it.
I bought it on iPhone — do I get the Mac app?
They are separate apps on the App Store and are purchased separately.
My history is not on my other device
By design. Nothing syncs, because nothing leaves the device it was written on.

Data and deletion

Everything Orakl holds is on the device you typed it into. There is no account to close and no server copy to request. Deleting the app removes all of it. The full text is in the privacy policy.

Orakl is a structured way to think through a decision. It is not financial, legal, medical or professional advice.
